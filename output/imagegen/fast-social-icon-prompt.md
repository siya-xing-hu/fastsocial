## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a production app icon for the Fast Social Chrome extension, a personal AI assistant for writing social posts, replying and translation.
Primary request: Create one fresh, highly legible icon replacing an old yellow/navy badge. A confident dark midnight-navy speech bubble with a single bold lightning bolt cut out of its center, on a warm vivid yellow rounded-square tile. The cutout bolt is the same yellow as the tile. The speech bubble should have a compact rounded rectangular silhouette with one short lower-left tail. Strong simple geometry, beautifully balanced optical proportions, thick forms that stay recognizable at 16 pixels.
Color palette: retain the existing project's sunshine yellow (#F9CE0B) and midnight navy (#0D1D35).
Style: refined flat vector-style brand mark rendered as a PNG, perfectly front-facing, crisp smooth edges, no outlines, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no dimensional rendering.
Composition: square 1024 by 1024 canvas; the yellow rounded-square tile occupies approximately 94% of the canvas with only a small evenly spaced transparent outside margin; the navy speech-bubble mark occupies about 64% of the tile and is optically centered; use a generously open, clearly defined lightning cutout. Rounded corners on tile about 22% corner radius.
Background: actual transparent pixels outside the rounded tile, no checkerboard printed into the image.
Constraints: deliver a single isolated icon only, no mockup, no presentation board, no variants, no text, no letters, no watermark, no bird, no extra sparkle, no little decoration.
